Replaces agy's `KTT_YOUTUBE_TITLE_HOOKS_29_TAP.md` for these episodes. The story is 27 tập (327 chương, see
`ktt-source-dedupe-261009-0900.md`); Tập 11–27 follow the same standard.

## Standard (user, 2026-10-08)

`<lời khen đúng sự thật>: <hook ngắn> - Kẻ Trừ Tà - Tập N - Liễu Ám Hoa Minh`

- The praise is optional. About half the episodes go without it, so the channel does not read as clickbait.
- Each praise line is used once and states a checked fact in words, with no score.
- The hook is a situation a new viewer understands. It does not give away the episode's answer.
- No comma and no "và". At most 100 characters (NFC).

Facts the praise lines may use (checked 2026-10-08 on Baidu Baike and book.douban.com):
- Serialized on Qidian (Khởi Điểm) and completed in 2007, about 1,386,697 characters.
- Printed as 3 books by 新世界出版社 in 2007. Book 1 is rated 8.2 on Douban (2,020 ratings), book 2 is rated 8.4.
- The series has a sequel, 驱魔人Ⅱ.
- Not usable: "8.3 Douban" (that score belongs to Blatty's *The Exorcist*), "Thiên Nhai" (that is Đạo Sĩ's
  platform), "20 triệu lượt đọc" and "Top 1" (no source), and "bán chạy / tái bản nhiều năm" (one blog line only).
